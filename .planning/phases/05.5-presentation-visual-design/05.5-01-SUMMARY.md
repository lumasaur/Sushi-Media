## Decisions Made

**1. Flexible layout over fixed height**
- Changed from `h-full flex items-center justify-center` to `min-h-full flex flex-col justify-center`
- Rationale: Allows content to grow naturally while maintaining centering for short content
- Added `autoScroll` prop (default: true) for explicit overflow control

**2. Red (beni) as accent only**
- Philosophy: "Red like wasabi - powerful in small amounts, overwhelming in large"
- Replaced Slide 2 `bg-beni` with `bg-premium-dark`
- Replaced Slide 7 `bg-beni` with `bg-deepIndigo`
- Rationale: Red backgrounds too aggressive, premium gradients more sophisticated

**3. Premium palette additions**
- Added: deepIndigo (#1A1F3A), warmCream (#F5F1E8), coral (#FF6B5A), softGold (#D4AF37), charcoal (#2D2D2D)
- Rationale: Provides sophisticated alternatives to heavy red usage
- Maintains Japanese aesthetic while adding visual variety

**4. Image organization structure**
- Structure: hero/ (1 image), main-story/ (4 images), mini-story/ (3 images)
- Rationale: Semantic categories match slide hierarchy and usage patterns
- WebP optimization deferred to future plan (Phase 05.5-04 or later)
